FoodRescue - Full-Stack Food Waste & Relief Platform

An end-to-end full-stack platform built to connect food donors, local volunteers, and partner NGOs to combat hunger and minimize food waste.

📍 Real-Time Leaflet Mapping: Interactive maps for live donor location discovery and volunteer dispatch.
🔐 Dual-Tier OTP Verification: Secure chain-of-custody verification ensuring food arrives reliably from donor to NGO.
⚡ Live WebSocket Tracking: Real-time tracking and instant status synchronization using Socket.IO.
🌱 ESG Environmental Impact Engine: Automated calculations of environmental metrics, meals served, and carbon emissions saved.
